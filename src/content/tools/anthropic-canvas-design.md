## Overview

The **canvas-design** skill is a distinct subproject of Anthropic's public skills repository. Unlike the **algorithmic-art** skill, its documented outputs are static graphics and PDFs. Treat the two as separate capabilities, and do not confuse them with the discontinued ChatGPT Canvas feature.
