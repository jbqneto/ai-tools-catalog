## What it does

[AutoClip](https://github.com/zhouxiaoka/autoclip) is a desktop application for preparing shorter videos from source material. The project documents a workflow where users provide a link or media input, choose a destination platform, and generate a clip together with related publishing assets such as a cover image and copy.

Its maintainers document desktop, CLI, and MCP interfaces, and support for formats associated with platforms including TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and other social-video services.

## Useful for

- Finding excerpts from podcasts, interviews, or long-form videos.
- Preparing vertical clips and captions for multiple publishing platforms.
- Exploring agent-controlled editing and media-production automation.

## Licensing and limitations

The repository declares the application **MIT licensed**. The maintainers distinguish the free desktop software from **cloud AI models billed by usage**; using the application does not necessarily make the entire processing workflow free or offline.

The quality and number of generated clips depend on the selected models and input material. Review the generated transcript, edit boundaries, captions, exports, and metadata before publication.

Only process media you have rights to use. Compliance with platform policies, source licensing, and the copyright status of clips is the user's responsibility. See the project's [repository](https://github.com/zhouxiaoka/autoclip) and [documentation](https://zhouxiaoka.github.io/autoclip_intro/).
