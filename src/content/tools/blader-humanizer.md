## Overview

Humanizer is a style-editing skill influenced by Wikipedia's guidance on common signs of AI-generated writing. It helps coding agents identify stock phrases, repetitive structures, exaggerated claims, and overly promotional tone.

**Typical uses:** polishing drafts for websites, product descriptions, announcements, and articles, especially when supplied with examples of the intended writing voice.

**Limitations:** This skill **does not verify factual claims** and does not guarantee passing AI detectors. Its repository explicitly says detector evasion is not its goal. Manual editorial judgment is required to preserve accuracy and author intent.

**Source:** [Official project repository](https://github.com/blader/humanizer).
