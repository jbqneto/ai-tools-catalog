## Overview

The project transcribes source media, proposes and scores candidate segments, asks the user which clips to produce, and supports platform-specific vertical exports with captions.

**Use cases:** extracting YouTube Shorts, Reels, and similar short-form clips from talks or podcasts.

**Caveats:** this workflow installs Python/Node dependencies and relies on FFmpeg, transcription, and video processing. Scoring is a heuristic, not evidence of future reach or retention. Verify all cuts and check rights before posting.
