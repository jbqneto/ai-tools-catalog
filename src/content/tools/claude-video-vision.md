Claude Video Vision documents a workflow for inspecting local videos through sampled frames and a local speech-to-text pipeline, producing time-referenced observations.

**Useful for:** preliminary video comprehension, indexing scenes, and answering questions about a local clip.

**Considerations:** this small community repository is not an official Claude product and has limited visible development history. Video analysis through sampled frames is not equivalent to reviewing every frame. Test reliability and permissions before use.
