## Overview

Claude Watch combines video downloading/inspection, selected frames, transcripts, and agent-written study notes, with outputs organized into a persistent collection.

**Use cases:** tutorial reviews, lecture notes, and studying screen-recorded demonstrations.

**Caveats:** accuracy is limited by speech transcription, frame sampling, and model interpretation. Recording source material may be subject to copyright or access rules. Validate important facts against the original video.
