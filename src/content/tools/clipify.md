Clipify uses transcription and editing scripts to propose excerpts, reframe talking-head footage, and generate subtitles for social media. Its README describes a local FFmpeg- and Whisper-based workflow optimized for dialogue-heavy videos.

**Useful for:** podcast excerpts, interview highlights, and short-form cuts from longer recordings.

**Considerations:** the documented workflow is optimized for macOS tooling, particularly VideoToolbox, and may require adaptation elsewhere. Clip selection and automatic reframing require human quality review.
