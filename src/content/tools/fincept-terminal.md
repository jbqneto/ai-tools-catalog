## What it does

Fincept Terminal brings market research, financial datasets, portfolio-related tools, and analytical workflows together in a desktop-oriented application. It is designed for interactive exploration rather than being a recommendation or guarantee of investment results.

## Useful for

- Exploring financial and economic data in a unified interface.
- Learning about data-driven investment research workflows.
- Evaluating integrations that connect external datasets, analysis modules, and AI services.
