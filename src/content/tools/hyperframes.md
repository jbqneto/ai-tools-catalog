HyperFrames lets developers and coding agents author animated scenes as web pages and render them into MP4 files. The project documents a CLI, agent skills, and timeline-driven rendering for motion graphics and product videos.

**Useful for:** code-driven videos, reusable motion graphics, animation experiments, and HTML/GSAP-based production pipelines.

**Considerations:** rendering may depend on a browser runtime, FFmpeg, and system resources. Review third-party audio, fonts, assets, and the repository's current license before distribution.
