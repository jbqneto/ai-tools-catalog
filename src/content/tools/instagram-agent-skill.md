## Overview

This project packages multiple Claude-oriented skills covering Reels hooks and scripts, carousel copy, captions, story plans, profile reviews, engagement drafts, and audits using data supplied by the user. It also includes local text-processing helpers.

**Typical uses:** preparing Instagram content plans, making scripts more specific, and reviewing results against actual account exports.

**Limitations:** The skills do not automatically publish material or intrinsically provide privileged Instagram analytics. Their scoring methods are heuristics, not guaranteed reach or conversion measures. Verify platform requirements and retain human approval before posting.
