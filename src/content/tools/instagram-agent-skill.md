## Overview

Instagram Agent Skill bundles **thirteen agent skills** for Reel scripting, hook evaluation, captions, carousels, weekly planning, account reviews, and other editorial tasks. It supplies local scripts and templates, including a configurable brand-voice reference.

## Useful for

- Developing reusable Instagram editorial workflows.
- Preparing Reels, carousels, captions, and content calendars.
- Reviewing proposed posts before publication.

## Licensing and limitations

**MIT** according to the repository. These are agent instructions and helper scripts—not a complete Instagram scheduling, analytics, or publishing service. The project explicitly leaves **posting to the user**, and some workflows require manually supplied examples or browser observations.

Scores from the included scripts are editorial heuristics, not proven predictors of engagement. Respect Meta's platform rules, the privacy of account data, and the rights to third-party media.
