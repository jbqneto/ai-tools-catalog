## Overview

The Motion Design Skill from LottieFiles provides an animation decision framework, motion principles, duration and easing references, and reusable patterns for entrances, transitions, feedback states, and choreography.

**Typical uses:** improving UI micro-interactions, making consistent motion systems for websites, and guiding agents generating CSS, GSAP, Lottie, or other animations.

**Limitations:** This is guidance for an AI coding agent, **not an animation editor or independent renderer**. The final quality depends on implementation, accessibility, and manual review. The separate Claude Motion Design and Motion Skills projects already in this catalog have different source repositories and are not aliases.

**Source:** [Official project repository](https://github.com/LottieFiles/motion-design-skill).
