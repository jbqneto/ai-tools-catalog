## Overview

Natron is a traditional visual-effects compositor. It can complement a code-driven video pipeline: render scenes separately, then combine footage, masks and effects in a node graph. The project documents a GPLv2 license and OpenFX support. The screenshot is a promotional illustration, not proof of the software's current UI.
