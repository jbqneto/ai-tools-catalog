## Overview

Omni Captions Skills groups workflows for generating timestamped transcripts, translating captions, converting subtitle formats, downloading authorized media, and correcting alignment.

**Use cases:** multilingual subtitle pipelines, video localization, and subtitle-file maintenance.

**Caveats:** integrations and translation backends have different service requirements. Subtitle timing and translation quality should be reviewed by a human, especially for names, technical vocabulary, and languages with sparse model support. Observe source-platform terms.
