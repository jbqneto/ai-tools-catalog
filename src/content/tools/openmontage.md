## Overview

OpenMontage combines multiple production paths, including footage-based montage, image-based composition, narration, graphics, and final MP4 exports. It documents open-source tools and optional integrations for various media sources.

**Use cases:** automated explainer videos, documentary montages, narrated visual stories, and production experiments.

**Caveats:** different workflows require different dependencies and possibly external service credentials. Publicly accessible footage does not automatically grant unrestricted reuse rights. Verify the current license, audio rights, media provenance, and the render before publication.
