## Overview

Pipecat is a Python framework for composing **real-time voice and multimodal conversational agents**. It connects audio/video transports with speech recognition, language models, speech synthesis, and other services. The project also documents client SDKs and multi-agent orchestration.

## Useful for

- Voice assistants, interactive customer support, and speech-first interfaces.
- Prototyping WebRTC/WebSocket voice agents with interchangeable providers.
- Implementing controlled, low-latency speech pipelines.

## Licensing and limitations

**BSD-2-Clause**, as declared by the official repository. This is a framework—not a turnkey call-center product or free inference service. Speech, LLM, telephony, and media providers may have separate charges and privacy requirements. Production systems need careful handling of consent, latency, failures, recordings, and access credentials.
