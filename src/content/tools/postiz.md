## Overview

[Postiz](https://postiz.com/) supports social-media scheduling, calendars, content management, collaboration and analytics. Its [official source repository](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app) describes an AGPL-3.0 self-hostable edition alongside its managed cloud offering.

## Deployment and capabilities

The self-hosted edition supports API and workflow integration, including n8n, but it involves provisioning and operating infrastructure and often registering social-network developer applications. The hosted and self-hosted options have different responsibilities and potential costs. AI-assisted composition and related functions are **optional**, not prerequisites for scheduling.

**Official documentation:** https://docs.postiz.com/
