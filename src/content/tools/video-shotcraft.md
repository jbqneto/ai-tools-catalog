Video Shotcraft describes a workflow where a coding agent captures the product's actual interface, selects storyboard and camera treatments, creates a Remotion composition, synchronizes cuts and sound, and delivers a rendered video. It also maintains reference shot recipes and a gallery of examples.

**Useful for:** product launches, landing-page walkthroughs, app showcases, and branded video demos.

**Considerations:** the project and the libraries it invokes have independent licensing and resource requirements. Outputs should be reviewed for real product accuracy, soundtrack rights, and legible text before publication.
