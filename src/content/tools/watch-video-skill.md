## Overview

Watch Video extracts a transcript from available captions or transcription services and samples video frames. It combines these with timestamps for a coding agent to inspect and summarize visual and spoken content.

**Use cases:** understanding tutorial videos, studying demonstrations, finding moments in videos, and generating structured notes.

**Caveats:** videos without usable captions may require a configured transcription API; processing can transmit audio to external providers. Review privacy and copyright implications. Extracted frames are samples, not complete continuous visual analysis.
