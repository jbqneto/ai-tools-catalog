Watch Video Skill aligns transcript segments with still frames from local or online videos and produces structured notes for coding assistants. It can reuse existing captions or rely on optional transcription services. This is an independent project, distinct from Claude Video Vision and Claude Watch. Sampling frames may miss fast action or visual changes.
