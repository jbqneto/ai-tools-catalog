YouTube Agent Skills covers scripting, hooks, titles paired with thumbnails, review of retention exports, clip ideas, comment triage, chapters and channel planning. It can analyze user-supplied YouTube metrics but does not magically access private channel analytics; provide exports or authorized integrations when needed. All publication decisions remain with the channel owner.
