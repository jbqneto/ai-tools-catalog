## Overview

yt-dlp is a feature-rich command-line media downloader. It offers format selection, metadata handling, subtitle options, media extraction, and integration with external tools such as FFmpeg.

## Useful for

- Obtaining permitted source recordings for a local media-analysis workflow.
- Downloading your own uploads or other authorized material.
- Feeding audio and video into transcription and video-processing tools.
