## Overview

yt-dlp downloads available media and extracts related metadata from a broad range of supported sites. It can be scripted as the acquisition step in local video processing and analysis pipelines.

**Typical uses:** downloading media you have permission to use, extracting metadata, creating local research datasets, and preparing assets for FFmpeg or transcription.

**Limitations:** Platform extractors require updates as websites change. Download availability is not a grant of copyright or permission. Respect platform terms, access controls, and rights in the source material. yt-dlp is a conventional media utility, **not an AI downloader or model**.

**Source:** [Official project repository](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp).
